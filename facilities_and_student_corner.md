# PART II: STUDENT CORNER 🎓

The Student Corner encompasses dynamic cells, clubs, and professional societies designed to foster community service, professional growth, entrepreneurship, and technical innovation.

## 1. National Service Scheme (NSS)
The NSS Unit at JNTU-GV CEV coordinates volunteer-led community service projects under the motto **"Not Me, But You"**.

* **Programme Officer**: V. Mani Kumar
* **Key Focus Areas**:
  * **Community Service**: Spreading health awareness, cleanliness, and digital literacy.
  * **NSS Special Camp**: An annual **7-day residential village service camp** focusing on rural health, dental check-ups, and sanitation drives.
  * **Social Drives**: Pulse Polio drives, blood donation camps, voter card registration campaigns, and Aadhaar enrollment camps.

### Annual 7-Day Residential Camp Schedule
| Day | Scheduled Activities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1** | Village Socio-Economic Survey & need assessment |
| **Day 2** | Sanitation program & Anganwadi school cleanliness drive |
| **Day 3** | Anti-open defecation awareness rally & pamphlet distribution |
| **Day 4** | Anti-alcoholism campaign & de-addiction awareness drives |
| **Day 5** | Free dental check-up camp & Blood Donation drive |
| **Day 6** | Motivational classes & basic literacy sessions for village children |
| **Day 7** | COVID/viral infection prevention awareness & concluding ceremony |

## 4. Professional Bodies & Student Chapters
Dynamic student-run chapters of global and national professional societies are hosted within core departments:

### 🌟 Computer Society of India (CSI)
* **About**: Established in 2014 with over 240+ student members. Dr. A. S. N. Chakravarthy acts as the Student Branch Coordinator.
* **Core Focus**: Facilitating knowledge sharing in computer science, holding programming contests, web development workshops, and cyber-security webinars.

### 🌟 IEEE Student Branch
* **About**: Connected to the electrical and electronics engineering sectors, advancing technology for the benefit of humanity.
* **Core Focus**: Poster presentations, national level symposia, and electrical grid modeling projects.

### 🌟 Institution of Engineers (India) (IE)
* **About**: Attached to the Department of Mechanical Engineering. Cooperates with the IE(I) Visakhapatnam Chapter.
* **Core Focus**: CAD/CAM design workshops, guest lectures on computational fluid dynamics (CFD), and structural engineering.

### 🌟 Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers (IETE)
* **About**: Managed under the Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE).
* **Core Focus**: Project expos, hardware exhibitions, PCB design training, and micro-controller design contests.

### 🌟 Indian Institute of Metals (IIM)
* **About**: Active affiliate chapter within Metallurgical Engineering.
* **Core Focus**: Material science webinars, metallurgy symposiums (**EISEN** series), and industry resource center access.

## 5. Industry-Institution Interaction Cell (IIPC)
Acts as a strategic conduit aligning engineering education with corporate expectations.

* **Key Functions**:
  * **MoUs**: Facilitates active Memorandums of Understanding with core manufacturing, information technology, and steel industries.
  * **Industrial Visits**: Coordinates student field trips to power plants, IT parks, and manufacturing units.
  * **Internships**: Assists in procuring semester-long internships and real-world capstone projects.
  * **Expert Guidance**: Invites industry leaders to deliver guest lectures on agile practices and cloud frameworks.
